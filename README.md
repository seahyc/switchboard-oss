# Switchboard

Let your agent search your messages and find details in PDFs or attachments shared in group chats months ago.

Ask it:

- "Where's the PDF someone sent about that project?"
- "What deadline was mentioned in the attachment?"
- "Who shared it, and which group was it in?"

Connect the accounts you choose. Switchboard keeps a local archive your agent can search and read, so you can ask a question instead of scrolling through months of chat history.

## Get started

1. [Download Switchboard](https://github.com/seahyc/switchboard-oss/releases).
2. [Connect WhatsApp or Slack](docs/SELF_HOSTING.md).
3. [Give your agent access](docs/AGENT_SETUP.md).

Available history and attachments depend on the connector. Slack capture is experimental and read-only; see [setup and limits](docs/CONNECTORS.md).

Your archive stays on your machine. See [privacy](docs/PRIVACY.md), [security](SECURITY.md), and [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
